# Owner.com company context

Last verified: 2026-08-05.

Owner.com is the AI platform independent restaurants use to grow direct
online sales: an AI-powered restaurant website, commission-free online
ordering, a branded mobile app, marketing automation (email, SMS, push,
loyalty/rewards), SEO and local listings, analytics, and POS integrations
(Square, Clover — it integrates rather than replaces). Its own current job
postings describe "the AI-native system local business owners use to succeed,
starting with restaurants," with the philosophy line: "Most small business
software makes owners do the work… Owner does the work for them agentically."
The 2025 product push is "AI Executives" — AI CMO/CFO/CTO agents acting for
restaurant owners.

Business model: flat $499/month per location, no tiers, zero commission. The
product is deliberately opinionated and rejects per-customer customization —
it wins through standardized templates A/B-tested across customers. The
company describes itself as "Shopify for local business owners, starting with
restaurants."

Funding and scale: Series C May 2025 — $120M at a $1B valuation (Meritech +
Headline), a 5x jump from the Jan 2024 $33M Series B (Redpoint + Altman
Capital); ~$180M raised in total. 10K+ restaurant locations at Series C;
current postings claim 20K+ owners assisted, $1B+ in online orders processed,
and ~$200M in fees saved. Headcount 350+ (CTO's site, undated). Remote-first
across the US and Canada, with an optional San Francisco Presidio office.

People and culture: founded by Adam Guild (CEO; COVID-era pivot from dine-in
SEO to commission-free ordering) and Dean Bloembergen (CTO; ex-Marble, YC
S19). Five stated values: obsess over customers; bring good energy; move
fast, deliver excellence ("Speed is the most important advantage that
startups have"); build as an Owner; scale yourself. 20+ former founders on
staff; leadership drawn from Shopify, DoorDash, Compass, HubSpot. Empathy for
independent restaurant owners is the emotional core of the company story.

Treat every figure above as company-stated at the verification date; re-check
the live site before repeating one as current fact.

Do not confuse this company with anything else named "Owner" — only
`owner.com` and its Ashby board at `jobs.ashbyhq.com/Owner` are in scope.

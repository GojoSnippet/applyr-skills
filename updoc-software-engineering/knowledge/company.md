# UpDoc company context

Last verified: 2026-07-29.

UpDoc is an early-stage healthcare technology startup founded by Stanford
physicians. It is building what it publicly describes as the first clinically
validated, physician-supervised AI agent that manages chronic diseases. Its
first product is an FDA-cleared software medical device with a patient-facing
large language model that acts between clinic visits under physician
supervision, integrating into existing EHR workflows. Public messaging frames
this as "agentic AI that acts where only physicians could," aimed at health
systems, patients, and payers dealing with chronic-disease capacity limits.

Co-founders: Sharif Vakili, MD, MBA, MS (CEO, primary care physician) and
Ashwin Nayak, MD, MS (CTO, physician-engineer). Founding investment led by
Polaris Partners. Publicly named investors/advisors include Andy Conrad
(General Partner, S32; former Verily CEO), Gary Swart (Operating Partner,
Polaris Partners), and physician advisors including a former FDA Commissioner
(Robert Califf) and a former American Diabetes Association Chief Medical
Officer (Robert Gabbay).

UpDoc's homepage names Mayo Clinic, Eli Lilly, American Diabetes Association,
Microsoft, UCSF, University of Minnesota, Highmark, Ada, and Hopscotch as
partners/press mentions. Treat these as company-stated relationships, not
verified customer contracts — attribute them to UpDoc and this verification
date, and re-check the live site before repeating them as current facts.

Use this context to explain fit only where approved candidate evidence

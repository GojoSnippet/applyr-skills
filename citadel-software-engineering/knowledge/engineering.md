# Citadel engineering context

Last verified: 2026-08-02.

Public interview-preparation sources describe Citadel engineering roles as
emphasizing production-grade, performance-sensitive systems: mastery of a
systems-level or high-performance language (C++ or Python are named most
often), understanding of memory management and how code interacts with the
underlying operating system, and algorithmic depth (coding rounds plus a
system-design round in the standard loop). These are third-party
characterizations of the role, not a specific job description's text — treat
them as general domain signal, not a verified requirement of any one
posting.

Use these as general problem-shape signals to connect with approved
candidate evidence:

- production systems with real reliability or performance constraints;
- distributed or high-throughput backend services;
- debugging and operating systems under real production load;
- measurable outcomes (latency, throughput, cost, reliability) tied to a
  system the candidate actually owned.

Never claim a technology, language proficiency, finance-domain background,
or performance achievement for the candidate unless it appears in approved
inputs. In particular, do not claim C++ proficiency or trading-systems
experience unless the candidate's own approved materials state it.

- Third-party interview-prep guides (Citadel SWE interview process,

# Citadel company context

Last verified: 2026-08-02.

Citadel is a global alternative investment firm founded in 1990, operating
across equities, fixed income, credit, commodities, and quantitative
strategies. Citadel Securities is a separate, affiliated market-making firm —
do not attribute its roles, culture notes, or postings to Citadel or vice
versa.

Public interview-preparation sources consistently describe Citadel's
engineering interview process as: an application review and recruiter
screen, an online assessment (algorithmic problems), a technical phone
screen, and an onsite loop with coding and system-design rounds. The final
round is commonly described as assessing fit with a performance-driven
culture — interviewers reportedly weight concrete ownership and outcomes
("what you owned, how outcomes changed because of your actions") over
polished narrative. These are third-party interview-prep characterizations,
not Citadel's own published statements — use them only to inform tone
(concrete, outcome-focused) in application answers, never as a verified fact
about the candidate or a guarantee of the process.

Use this context to explain fit only where approved candidate evidence
connects to production systems, performance/reliability engineering, backend
infrastructure, or measurable technical outcomes.

# Build the private candidate evidence graph

Read `references/candidate-evidence.md` and `references/workspace-contract.md`.
The resume is an index, not a complete biography.

1. Inventory candidate-authorized career documents, project repositories,
   project documentation, architecture notes, tests, and prior confirmed
   answers. Ignore dependency caches, generated files, vendored code, secrets,
   and unrelated repositories.
2. Establish attribution before inferring capability. Prefer candidate-authored
   code, commits, design documents, tests, and direct project descriptions.
3. Extract atomic evidence records: capability, action, artifact, technology,
   scope, outcome, provenance, confidence basis, and publication state.
4. A dependency or configuration entry proves exposure, not mastery. Search for
   substantive authored use before strengthening the capability.
5. Public information about a former employer supplies company context only. It
   never proves the candidate performed a task or owned a system.
6. Classify every record as `publishable`, `private_context`,
   `needs_confirmation`, or `prohibited`. Redact secrets and proprietary data
   before producing even a publishable abstraction.
7. Detect contradictions across dates, titles, metrics, and project ownership.
   Preserve both sources and hold the claim from publication until resolved.
8. Version the evidence graph and record content hashes. Do not mutate an older
   version used by a submitted application.

Inference is allowed when authored work demonstrates the capability. Never
infer a metric, employer, date, title, personal ownership, production
deployment, team size, business impact, or regulated-domain experience without
direct evidence.

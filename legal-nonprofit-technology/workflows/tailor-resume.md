# Tailor the resume aggressively and truthfully

Read `references/candidate-evidence.md`, `references/quality-gates.md`, the
immutable job description, evidence matrix, canonical resume, and the exact
candidate-evidence version recorded in job state.

1. Start from the canonical JakeResume source, never from another job's resume.
2. Select the strongest `publishable` evidence for the role, including relevant
   project evidence absent from the canonical resume.
3. Reorder sections and bullets around the role's actual priorities. Rewrite
   substantially when that makes ownership, technical depth, decisions, and
   outcomes clearer, but keep every clause traceable to evidence ids.
4. Use terminology from the job only when it accurately names demonstrated
   work. Avoid keyword stuffing, vague superlatives, copied job-description
   phrasing, and synthetic corporate language.
5. Do not publish `private_context`, `needs_confirmation`, or `prohibited`
   material. Generalize confidential implementation details only when a
   publishable evidence record explicitly permits the abstraction.
6. Preserve identity, employers, titles, dates, education, and verified metrics.
   Never create personal ownership, production deployment, or business impact.
7. Produce a visually complete one- or two-page resume. Prefer one full page
   when the evidence fits; use two pages when strong relevant evidence warrants
   it. Half-empty pages, clipped content, tiny type, or destructive compression
   fail review.
8. Compile the PDF, extract its text, render every page, and save the source,
   PDF, evidence map, and change summary in this job workspace.

Return artifacts only after `workflows/review-application.md` passes them.

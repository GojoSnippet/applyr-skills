# Research one organization and job

Read `references/candidate-evidence.md`, `references/source-strategy.md`,
`references/quality-gates.md`, and the exact immutable job snapshot.

1. Confirm the job is active and its content hash still matches the claimed
   version. Reconcile a changed repost before continuing.
2. Reuse a fresh read-only organization dossier when available. Research only
   gaps that affect fit, writing, or application answers.
3. Understand the organization's legal identity, mission, programs, users,
   funding context, technology needs, and public engineering signals. Separate
   facts from reasonable hypotheses.
4. Parse the job into hard requirements, responsibilities, preferred signals,
   mission/domain context, and likely first-year outcomes.
5. Build an evidence matrix mapping each meaningful requirement to exact
   candidate evidence ids. Mark genuine gaps; do not fill them with synonyms.
6. Identify the three to five strongest interview themes and the evidence that
   supports each one.
7. Store research only in this job's `research/` directory. Organization facts
   eligible for reuse may be proposed to the organization dossier after they
   are source-cited and stripped of job-specific conclusions.

Reject the job when a hard gate fails or expected value no longer justifies an
application. Otherwise hand the exact evidence matrix to tailoring and review.

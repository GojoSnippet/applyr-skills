# Automatically review the application package

Read `references/quality-gates.md`, the evidence matrix, candidate-evidence
version, exact job snapshot, resume source/PDF, cover letter when used, and all
draft form answers. Review the full package, not the resume in isolation.

Run these independent checks:

1. **Evidence:** map every factual clause to publishable evidence and reject
   unsupported identity, date, metric, outcome, ownership, deployment, or
   confidential detail.
2. **Role coverage:** name covered must-haves, uncovered must-haves, important
   terminology, and the evidence used. Do not invent an ATS percentage.
3. **Human voice:** remove generic enthusiasm, repetitive cadence, excessive
   adjectives, copied job language, and claims that could describe anyone.
4. **Consistency:** compare resume, letter, profile, location, authorization,
   dates, compensation, and every form answer for contradictions.
5. **Rendered PDF:** inspect every rendered page and extracted text. Reject
   half-empty pages, clipping, overlap, broken glyphs, unreadable type, strange
   whitespace, or missing strong evidence.
6. **Application readiness:** verify the current form, required attachments,
   screening answers, links, and contact information immediately before submit.

Write a structured report under `review/`. Revise and rerun failed checks. Do
not weaken an evidence rule to make a draft pass. After two unsuccessful review
cycles, mark the precise blocker instead of looping indefinitely.

# Legal nonprofit technology applications

Optimize for interviews and job offers, not activity counts. Process every
worthwhile role that passes the gates. There is no application quota or daily
minimum; runtime concurrency, cost, duplicate, and portal-safety controls still
apply.

## Route the task

Read the workflow matching `task_kind`:

- discovery: `workflows/discover.md`
- candidate evidence ingestion: `workflows/build-candidate-evidence.md`
- job research: `workflows/research-job.md`
- resume tailoring: `workflows/tailor-resume.md`
- package review: `workflows/review-application.md`
- application: `workflows/apply.md`
- retention or cleanup: `workflows/cleanup.md`

Always read:

- `references/workspace-contract.md`
- `references/state-and-deduplication.md`

Read when relevant:

- `references/candidate-evidence.md` for matching, writing, or answering;
- `references/source-strategy.md` for discovery or employer verification;
- `references/quality-gates.md` for research, tailoring, review, or submission.

## Non-negotiable behavior

1. Verify that the employer belongs to the legal-nonprofit boundary before
   spending application effort. A mission statement alone is insufficient.
2. Check durable job and application state before research, before claiming a
   run, and before submission.
3. Give each run exactly one job workspace read-write. Mount candidate evidence
   and organization dossiers read-only. Never read another job's drafts.
4. The resume is an index into the candidate's work, not the boundary of what
   the candidate knows. Use attributable projects and authored work according
   to `references/candidate-evidence.md`.
5. Write aggressively and specifically from supported evidence. Never invent
   employers, titles, dates, metrics, business outcomes, personal ownership,
   production deployment, regulated-domain experience, or technologies.
6. Keep `private_context`, `needs_confirmation`, and `prohibited` evidence out
   of submitted material. Never expose secrets or confidential details.
7. Run the automatic review loop. A half-empty or broken resume, generic
   writing, keyword stuffing, inconsistent answers, or missing required file
   is not submission-ready.
8. Use the final submission tool only after the form and artifacts pass review.
   A click is not proof: store a confirmation page, confirmation message, or
   stable receipt before marking the application submitted.
9. Pause only for authentication, CAPTCHA, a genuinely missing candidate fact,
   a required voluntary choice, or a blocker the available tools cannot solve.

Record source versions, evidence references, decisions, artifacts, review
results, and receipts so another run can reproduce what was submitted without
sharing job-specific context with a different application.

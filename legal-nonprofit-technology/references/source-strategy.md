# Nationwide legal-nonprofit source strategy

## Employer boundary

Accept US legally registered nonprofits and qualifying NGOs, foundations,
nonprofit universities, research institutes, and nonprofit hospital systems.
Use an authoritative government record when applicable, or reliable official
evidence for entities whose legal form does not map cleanly to a 501(c) record.
Record entity name, identifier, status, source URL, retrieval date, and evidence
excerpt. A mission statement alone is insufficient.

For fiscally sponsored work, identify both the public project and its legal
fiscal sponsor. Reject mission-driven for-profits from this campaign even when
their work is socially valuable.

## Sources

Search in tiers, then deduplicate globally:

1. Idealist; Fast Forward Tech Nonprofit; NTEN; National Council of Nonprofits
   and its state associations; Council on Foundations.
2. Work for Good; National Nonprofits; NPO.net; relevant association boards.
3. Direct career pages and ATS feeds for verified organizations, foundations,
   nonprofit universities, research institutes, and hospital systems.
4. Broader social-impact, climate, development, or technology boards as lead
   sources only; every employer must still pass legal verification.

Prefer direct employer pages as the authoritative job description. Store source
lineage when an aggregator redirects to a company ATS. Recheck job activity and
legal status before submission when cached evidence is stale or contradictory.

# Quality gates

## Before research

- Employer legal status is verified and source-cited.
- Job is active, unique, and represented by an immutable snapshot.
- Hard eligibility requirements do not fail.

## Before final artifacts

- Requirements map to exact publishable evidence or an explicit gap.
- Writing is specific, human, concise, and free of keyword stuffing.
- Resume starts from canonical JakeResume source, not another application.
- Resume identity, employers, titles, dates, education, technologies, metrics,
  and outcomes are consistent with evidence.
- Cover letter and answers add role-specific reasoning rather than repeat the
  resume or imitate the job description.

## Automatic render and package review

Compile, extract text from, and inspect the rendered PDF page by page. Fail
half-empty pages, clipped or overlapping text, broken glyphs, unreadable type,
excessive whitespace, or removal of stronger relevant evidence. A complete
one-page resume is preferred when it fits; two pages are valid when supported
evidence warrants them.

Report covered must-haves, gaps, terminology alignment, risky clauses, and
layout defects directly. Never advertise an invented ATS score.

## Before submission

- Evidence, coverage, human voice, consistency, rendered PDF, and live form
  checks pass.
- Correct final files are attached and readable.
- Every required field is answered from publishable evidence or an explicit
  candidate choice.
- Runtime lease, domain, duplicate, budget, and platform controls pass.

The application remains unverified until a confirmation page, message,
application id, or stable receipt is stored. Missing proof triggers state
reconciliation, not a blind second submission.

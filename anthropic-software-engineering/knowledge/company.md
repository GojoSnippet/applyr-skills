# Anthropic — company context

Last verified: 2026-08-02.

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company. Its stated mission is to
ensure that transformative AI has a positive impact on humanity — building
AI systems (the Claude model family) that are reliable, interpretable, and
steerable, with safety treated as a core engineering discipline rather than
an afterthought. The company developed Constitutional AI, a method for
training models to be helpful, honest, and harmless using an explicit,
written set of principles rather than relying solely on human feedback.
Source: [anthropic.com/constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution),
[anthropic.com/news/core-views-on-ai-safety](https://www.anthropic.com/news/core-views-on-ai-safety).

Third-party characterizations (used only for tone guidance, not verified
company fact): coverage consistently frames Anthropic as distinguishing
itself from other frontier AI labs by treating safety and interpretability
research as integral to product development rather than a separate
compliance function.
Sources: [Medium — Pioneering AI Safety and Innovation](https://bytebridge.medium.com/anthropic-pioneering-ai-safety-and-innovation-28da9172a50d),
[Medium — Redefining AI with Safety at Its Core](https://medium.com/@vvrr.17.4/anthropic-the-company-redefining-ai-with-safety-at-its-core-1874f89a8c48).

## Tailoring implications

- Favor language about building trustworthy, reliable systems and rigorous
  engineering practice over hype-driven "move fast" framing.
- Genuine interest in AI safety, interpretability, or responsible-AI
  practice is worth surfacing if the candidate's evidence actually supports
  it — never invent it.
- Never claim AI/ML research experience, publications, or a
  machine-learning research background unless approved candidate inputs
  say so; most open engineering roles are infrastructure/tooling positions
  supporting research and production systems, not research-scientist roles.

# Anthropic — engineering context for tailoring

Last verified: 2026-08-02, drawn from live job postings on
`job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic`.

## What the open engineering roles actually emphasize

Reading a representative sample of posted titles (Performance Engineer;
Software Engineer, Research Infrastructure; Software Engineer, RL Data;
Staff Software Engineer, Environments Infrastructure; Staff+ Software
Engineer, Safeguards ML Infrastructure; Research Engineer, Agents/Code
RL/Computer Use/Pretraining), the engineering org's work centers on:

- Infrastructure and tooling that supports large-scale ML training and
  reinforcement-learning workflows (data pipelines, training environments,
  evaluation/RL tooling).
- Performance and systems engineering (GPU performance, distributed
  systems at scale).
- Safety/safeguards engineering — tooling and infrastructure for model
  safety review, abuse/account-compromise detection, and review tooling.
- Human-facing research tooling (research tools, human-data interfaces).

This is engineering IN SERVICE OF ML research and production systems, not
primarily ML-research-scientist work — most of these roles want strong
generalist or infrastructure/backend engineering skill, not a research
publication record.

## Tailoring guidance

- Prioritize evidence of: distributed systems, large-scale/high-throughput
  infrastructure, data pipelines, backend services (Python/Go/Rust/C++),
  developer tooling, CI/build systems, and measurable performance or
  reliability improvements.
- Ownership of a system end-to-end, working effectively with ambiguous or
  fast-changing requirements, and cross-team collaboration are all worth
  surfacing when evidenced.
- Do not claim: published ML research, an academic research background,
  specific familiarity with reinforcement learning internals, or
  interpretability research experience, unless approved candidate inputs
  actually support it. A strong systems/infrastructure background does not
  need to be dressed up as ML-research experience to be a credible fit for
  most of these roles.

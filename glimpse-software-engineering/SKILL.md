# Glimpse software engineering applications

Apply this guidance only to the **Software Engineer** and **Senior Software
Engineer** roles at https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/glimpse. Identify the employer as
Glimpse at https://www.tryglimpse.com: a New York City AI platform for consumer
packaged-goods financial operations. Reject facts, jobs, stacks, or interview
reports for `glimp.se`, battery CT/imaging companies, VR/AR companies, The
Glimpse Group, and any other similarly named employer.

## Use evidence deliberately

Separate company evidence from candidate evidence in every answer.

- **Confirmed** means an official Glimpse page or current Glimpse job posting
  states it.
- **Corroborated** means a credible external company or investor source supports
  it. Attribute growth and funding claims; treat them as company claims, never
  as candidate achievements.
- **Unknown/inferred** means it is not publicly established. Label it plainly;
  do not turn it into a fact.

Use candidate facts only when they appear in the provided résumé, profile,
approved answer store, or user-provided corpus. Do not infer or invent tenure,
named-stack use, LLM/agent experience, metrics, leadership, mentoring, CPG or
financial-domain experience, New York location, relocation willingness, or US
work authorization. State required unknowns as questions or placeholders. Use
adjacent, evidenced strengths—such as full-stack ownership, data pipelines,
document processing, workflow automation, reliability, or learning speed—when
direct overlap is absent.

## Ground the company and fit

Use the following **confirmed** product context when it helps tailor an answer:
Glimpse supports deductions management, revenue recovery, cash application, and
retailer/ERP integrations. Its AI-agent workflows ingest and reconcile PDFs,
spreadsheets, HTML, remittance data, shipping and promotional records, and
retailer data. The public roles signal Python, Node.js, Next.js, MongoDB,
PostgreSQL, and LLM-enabled systems, alongside full-stack delivery, production
ownership, rapid iteration, debugging, cross-functional communication, and
comfort with ambiguity. The roles describe a five-day, on-site New York City
working model. The current job postings report that Glimpse launched this
product in April 2024, serves 200+ customers, and has raised $52M from investors
including a16z, 8VC, and Y Combinator; use these only as dated,
company-reported context.

For either role, connect only supported candidate evidence to the product's
messy-data, financial-workflow, and customer-facing engineering problems.

- For **Software Engineer**, emphasize at least three years of evidenced
  full-stack work, implementation speed, end-to-end delivery, and hands-on
  problem solving. Flag an unverified experience total rather than assuming it.
- For **Senior Software Engineer**, emphasize at least six years of evidenced
  experience plus architecture, scalability, technical leadership, production
  judgment, mentoring, and engineering-quality improvements. Treat the six-year
  threshold as a job requirement, not a claim to manufacture. If the candidate
  falls short, say so honestly and frame only demonstrated senior-level scope.

## Prepare the application, but do not submit it

The currently observed Software Engineer form asks for name, email, résumé,
whether the candidate is in New York City or willing to relocate, whether the
candidate is authorized to work in the US without current or future sponsorship,
a 150–200-word proudest-professional-accomplishment response, and a response on
what the candidate uniquely brings to Glimpse and the role.

Leave relocation and authorization unanswered when they are unknown. For the
accomplishment response, use: context and stakes → candidate-owned action →
verified result → why it mattered. For unique contribution, use two or three
grounded strengths → evidence for each → a careful connection to Glimpse's
engineering problems and working style. Do not claim that a candidate has used
Glimpse's stack or AI systems unless the record supports it. Stop at Applyr's
normal review/submission gate.

## Prepare for interviews without inventing a process

No reliable public candidate account establishes Glimpse's current interview
stages. State that the public loop is **unknown**; never describe a stage,
coding exercise, or question as reported fact unless it is confirmed for this
company and role.

Offer these **inferred preparation topics**, drawn from the job descriptions and
product architecture: full-stack/API design; Python, Node.js, and Next.js
tradeoffs; MongoDB/PostgreSQL modeling; ingesting and normalizing messy
documents and external data; LLM evaluation, reliability, observability, and
human review; production debugging and partial failure; multi-tenant financial
automation system design; and examples of rapid delivery, ownership, ambiguity,
and customer-driven decisions. Recommend asking the recruiter for the actual
stages and evaluation areas.
